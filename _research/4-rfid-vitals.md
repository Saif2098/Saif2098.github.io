title: "Vital Sign Sensing with Passive RFID Tags"

collection: research
tier: earlier
order: 4

An LSTM model estimates respiratory patterns from phase changes in backscattered signals off commercial UHF RFID tags, extending earlier WiFi CSI-based respiration sensing to mobile individuals using passive backscatter.
