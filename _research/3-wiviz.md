title: "WiViz"
excerpt: "An interactive 3D system that reveals the hidden mechanisms behind wireless signal strength and connectivity, helping non-experts reason about wireless behavior rather than just observe it."

Most users only see wireless outcomes — signal bars, coverage maps, connection quality — without an understandable account of why they occurred. WiViz places wireless information into a spatial model of the environment, built on NVIDIA Sionna RT, so people can connect observed outcomes with the physical causes behind them: device placement, obstacles, reflections, and multipath. In an earlier between-subjects study, participants using WiViz identified the correct underlying mechanism far more often than with a standard signal-strength heatmap (approximately 80.6% vs. 24.2%), and made more accurate decisions overall (87.5% vs. 65.2%). Current work studies whether these interactions improve wireless literacy, mechanistic reasoning, and transfer to unfamiliar scenarios.
